Demo: one Puck.js and your PC

Only one Puck.js? Here it joins a network simulated in the browser. The program really runs on the Puck, with its button, light and temperature sensors; its neighbours are virtual.

  1. Close the Espruino Web IDE, or disconnect it: the Puck accepts one connection at a time.
  2. Connect and pick the Puck (Chrome or Edge).
  3. Choose an example and press Upload to Puck: it installs the library and the program in a few seconds.
  4. Play with the real Puck: press its button, cover it, warm it in your hand. Drag the Puck node around the network and watch its LEDs.

How it works


On the Puck the program starts with target: 'host': instead of using the radio by itself, it waits for the PC. Every round the page sends it the messages of the simulated neighbours in range; the Puck runs the round with its own sensors and answers with its value and message, which the neighbours get in the next round. To the program, it is a real network.


The Puck reads sense('source') (its button toggles it), sense('light') (0 dark to 1 bright) and sense('temp') (°C). The simulated nodes get the same sensors from config.js. The LEDs show the Puck's value: red 0, green 1 or true, blue 2.


After changing program.js, upload again: devices only understand each other when they run the same program.


This demo tests your program on the real interpreter and in conversation with other nodes. The radio between two boards, the ble target, needs at least two devices.
